01The game

Players arrive as Hotel Eden's maintenance man. Daily repairs, conversations, and practical help build relationships with staff and guests, improve the retreat, and gradually expose the Eden family's occult history.

  • Place: an isolated mountain hotel surrounded by redwood forest, lakes, and wilderness.
  • Experience: intimate, atmospheric, elegant, and unsettling.
  • Core loop: maintain the hotel; build relationships; unlock opportunities; follow supernatural clues.
  • Simulation: NPC routines, relationships, and memory supported by Convai and a custom backend.
