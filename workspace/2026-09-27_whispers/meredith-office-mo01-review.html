Meredith's Office

Meredith's lake office, built in the MS02 master after your reference, behind the Executive Corridor's carved doors.

  • Fireplace: a white marble chimneypiece in a herringbone-brick firebox. Above it hang the large portrait, a brass picture light and two of the corridor's sconces. On the mantel are a pair of candelabra, five books and a clock.
  • Bookcases: one in the alcove beside the fireplace, and a full mahogany bookcase wall with a library ladder. Both are filled with period books.
  • Windows: each has an arched architrave, a single velvet swag with pleated tails and a gold fringe, sage curtains and ivory sheers.
  • Walls and floor: a mahogany dado, olive-grey damask paper and a deep cornice; herringbone parquet with a large Turkish rug.
  • Corner: the lobby's stair palm, at its own size, in the corner across from the door.

Everything is a game mesh. The carved mantel, the drapery, the candelabra and the clock are high-poly sources baked into low-poly meshes.

Finished and in the game. The office is installed in Unreal (L_EdenE2K, through the hotel level), with its lights, fire and simple colliders, and furnished with your approved desk, chair and chesterfield. Meredith sits and works at the desk. See Furniture and In the game. The later sections are the Blender renders of the same game meshes.
