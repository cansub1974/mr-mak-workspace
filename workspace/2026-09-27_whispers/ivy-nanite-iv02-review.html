Reproducible capture requirements

Five fixed views: first-person, oblique, courtyard, distant and dense overlap/shadow. Freeze transforms/FOV, resolution, scalability, render percentage, upscaler, lighting/exposure and scene state. Disable VSync/frame caps; finish shaders and warm streaming before ≥120 s steady captures, three comparable runs per configuration/view. Save separate first-use and warm traversal/streaming runs so warm-up does not conceal stalls.

Retain raw CSV, Insights traces, GPU profiles and available memory/streaming counters. Report frame/GPU/game/render distributions, per-run variation, p95/p99, pass costs and paired millisecond deltas. Identify CPU/GPU bound cases and unsupported counters. Keep screenshots/visualizations and profiler overhead separate from primary timings. Do not linearly extrapolate patch cost.

Rendering and visual comparisons

Standard Nanite, opaque geometric leaves, no WPO. Actual meshes cast VSM shadows. Compare Preserve Area on/off and opaque Default Lit/Two Sided Foliage (or verified Substrate equivalents) under front/back/local lights. Inspect leaf retention, temporal shimmer/popping, Nanite overdraw, layering, shadow detail, intersections and repetition. No fallback/VSM proxy confusion or physically correct transmission claim. Simplified shadow casters and localized POM are conditional profiling experiments, not defaults.

Gated kit and PCG continuation

After prototype acceptance: wall patches with sparse edges/transitions, corner-anchored pillar wraps, top-pivot hanging dangles and shared material instances. PCG uses explicit facade inclusion and window/door exclusions, directed surface queries, growth-up orientation, footprint support checks, distinct wall/corner/ledge rules, stable seeds, spacing/overlap limits, appropriate instancing/bounds/culling. Rigid patches do not conform from a single surface normal. Re-run the suite on final materials and PCG coverage.
