BCW and the Ascent: User Manual

Bingo Collaborative Workspace V0.17 with the Ascent AI server. For everyone who uses BCW day to day. November 2026.

1. What this system is

BCW (Bingo Collaborative Workspace) is the project's shared workspace. You open it in a web browser. It brings together the project's tasks, calendar, risk records, reports, files, people and equipment. It also gives every user a private AI Assistant and a searchable Library of standards, procedures and reports.

Everything runs on the site's own network, with no internet and no cloud services. Your documents and conversations never leave the site.

  • HAL3000 is the site PC that runs BCW. It stores every record, chat and approval.
  • The Ascent is a separate AI computer. It runs:
    • the Assistant's AI model (Inkling 2 Mini);
    • the Library's smart search;
    • document reading, including scanned pages in English and Traditional Chinese;
    • image making.
    It stores nothing of yours.
  • Mother is the shared document store. The Library reads its documents from there.

If the Ascent is switched off, BCW still works: tasks, calendar, files and records are all available. Only the AI features wait until it is back: the Assistant, smart Library search, reading new scanned documents, and images.

2. Getting started

Signing in

  1. Open BCW in your browser at https://192.168.2.35, or with the shortcut your administrator gave you. If the browser warns that the connection isn't private, your laptop hasn't been set up to trust BCW yet: ask the ICPM.
  2. Enter your Username and Password, then press Enter workspace →.
  3. To sign out, use the arrow (↪) under your name at the bottom of the left-hand menu.

Your password is the same for BCW, Nextcloud and OpenProject. Change it under Tools → My account → Change my password. The change applies to all three.

Some people sign in with a laptop account (BMT01 to BMT08). These accounts pass from one occupant to the next. Your administrator sets who currently holds each one.

Project weeks

BCW counts time in Project Weeks. A week runs Sunday to Saturday, and Week 1 began on 16 November 2025. The current week is shown at the top of Today. Reports, deliverables and calendar filters all use these week numbers.

3. Today

Today is your start-of-day page. It shows:

  • The project week, with this week's Monday-to-Friday reporting dates.
  • Shortcut cards for Tasks, Sailing readiness, Weekly reporting and Resources (only those you may open).
  • Your priorities: up to five of your tasks. View all tasks opens the full list.
  • Sailing attention: a summary of open MCM risks for the next sailing. It is a reminder, not a readiness or clearance decision.
  • Your routines and overdue reminders, with Done, Skip and Snooze 1 hour.

If a banner says "Some sources are unavailable. Displayed records may be cached.", one of the connected systems is briefly unreachable. Press Refresh later.

4. The Assistant

The Assistant is your own AI helper. Your chats are private to you. It runs on the Ascent with the Inkling 2 Mini model and can work with BCW's records, the Library, your files and images.

Why does it say "Codex"? Codex is the name of the software that drives the Assistant. The send button reads Send to Codex and replies are labelled "Codex", but the thinking is done by Inkling 2 Mini on the Ascent. Nothing is sent outside the site.

Starting and managing chats

  • New session starts a fresh chat. Use a new chat for each new topic: it keeps answers focused and fast.
  • Type in the Request box and press Send to Codex. Drafts save themselves ("Draft saved"), and Save draft saves now.
  • The chat list offers Recent chats, All chats, Archived chats and Deleted chats. Inside a chat: Rename, Archive, Delete chat and Restore.
  • Interrupt stops an answer that is going the wrong way.
  • You can close the page while it works. The work carries on and the answer is waiting when you come back.
  • Assistant capabilities and limits shows the current model and the skills it has.

Approving the Assistant's proposals

When the Assistant wants to change something in BCW, a card appears: "Awaiting your decision". Read it, then press Approve once or Decline. You can also just reply "yes" or "no". Past proposals are listed under Proposal history.

The Assistant cannot change MCM records or existing Mother files, and it cannot act without your approval.

Adding files to a chat

  1. Open Files and execution and choose Add a file to this session.
  2. The file shows "Processing…", then "Ready — text read". You can now ask about it.
  3. If it shows Failed, press Retry reading. Very large Office files read better split into parts.

Files the Assistant makes are listed under Downloads. For spreadsheets, Preview BCW records shows what would be imported before anything changes.

Images

  • Image requests join a queue. A Your image jobs panel above the chat shows progress. When the image is ready, use View or Download. It also appears in the chat's Downloads.
  • An image normally takes about a minute, and you can keep chatting meanwhile.
  • You can have up to five image jobs waiting at once.
  • New images are made with the Krea2 model. Edits to an existing picture use Qwen Image Edit. Describe what you want plainly; the Assistant writes the detailed instructions.

Skills

Skills are reviewed instruction sets that teach the Assistant a particular job, such as preparing a PLOW or writing in a set format. Open Skills to browse them:

  • Inspect skill shows what a skill does;
  • Enable skill turns it on for you;
  • Use this skill applies it to the current chat.

Getting the best answers

  • Be specific. Name the document, the week, the system or the person.
  • Ask for citations when the answer matters ("cite the procedure and page").
  • Check before you rely on it. Open the cited page for anything safety- or contract-related. The Assistant can be wrong, and engineers remain responsible for engineering decisions.
  • One topic per chat. Very long chats get slower.
  • The Ascent writes two answers at once. If two colleagues are already busy, your request waits its turn, usually briefly.

5. The Library

Project → Library is the site's searchable collection of engineering references and reports. It covers standards, specifications, procedures, manuals, drawings, and received and issued reports. BCW only reads these files. It never moves, renames or deletes them.

Searching

  1. Open the Search tab.
  2. Type words, a question or a document number in Search the library. Optionally pick a Category. Tick Include superseded revisions to see older revisions too.
  3. Press Search library. Each result shows the document number, revision, title, status and page.
  4. Open original at page N opens the source document at the right page.

Examples: "hull plating minimum thickness", "PRO-0007", "what torque for the main engine holding-down bolts".

Search understands meaning, not just exact words. If you see a notice that search is using words only, the Ascent's search service is unreachable. Results still work, just less cleverly.

Document status

StatusMeaning
currentThe latest revision. This is the one to use.
supersededAn older revision. Still searchable, and clearly labelled.
uncertainA Library editor needs to sort it out. One of these applies:
  • the file name or folder doesn't follow the standard;
  • two files claim the same number and revision;
  • the file changed without a new revision number.
awaiting approvalA standard, specification or procedure that nobody has approved yet. You can see it, but the Assistant and Preliminary Review don't use it until it is approved.
missing / unavailableThe file has gone from its folder, or the folder can't be reached.

Adding a document

  1. Name the file DocNo_Rev_Title, for example PRO-0007_R01_Tank inspection procedure.docx. Revisions are written R00, R01… or E plus the edition year (e.g. E2024).
  2. Put it in the right folder of Mother's Library folder. You can reach it through Tools → Files → Mother documents.
    • 01 Standards · 02 Specifications · 03 Procedures · 04 Manuals · 05 Drawings · 06 Reports\<Subject>
  3. BCW finds it within 15 minutes. A Library editor can press Scan now to find it at once.
  4. Standards, specifications and procedures then wait for approval by a Library editor.

BCW reads:

  • PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text and Markdown files;
  • scanned PDFs and images. Scanned pages are read on the Ascent, in English and Traditional Chinese.

Files over 50 MB are not read.

New revision, new file. When a document is revised, add the new revision as a new file with the new revision number. BCW then marks the old one superseded. Don't overwrite the old file: a changed file with the same revision number becomes "uncertain".

Preliminary Review

Preliminary Review asks the AI to check a report against the approved references and earlier reports. It looks for inconsistencies, missing content and contradictions before the report goes out.

  1. Open Preliminary Review, use Find a report to review, then Find reports.
  2. Press Review this report. The review runs in the background, typically for a minute or two, and the list updates by itself.
  3. Press Open findings. Each finding is labelled Inconsistency, Missing content, Contradiction or For engineer attention. It quotes the report passage and the reference passage, each with an Open original link.
  4. For each finding, add an Engineer note if useful, then press Accept or Dismiss. Reopen undoes a decision.

A review with no findings is not an approval. It means the AI found nothing to flag. The engineer's own review still decides.

6. Project work

Tasks

Project → Tasks shows the project's tasks and milestones, which live in OpenProject.

  • Filter by Work-package type (All, Tasks, Milestones) or use Search tasks….
  • New task and New milestone create items. Open an item to Edit it, Prioritize it or open it in OpenProject ↗.
  • Saving sends the change to OpenProject. If someone else changed the task meanwhile, the save is refused. Reopen it and make your change again.

Deliverables

Project → Deliverables tracks items with exact due dates and their due project week.

  • New deliverable takes a title, an owner and a delivery due date. Link OpenProject task connects it to a task.
  • Mark delivered, Cancel deliverable and Reopen change its state. Tick Include completed and cancelled to see finished items.
  • Preparing a report doesn't mark it delivered. The ICPM does that here once it has actually gone out.

Sailing readiness (MCM)

Project → Sailing readiness shows the MCM/MATSAFE engineering-risk database.

  • Its tabs are Readiness overview, SWBS systems, Components, Defect notifications, General risks, Event assessments and Mitigations.
  • By default it shows open Surface Trial assessments rated A or B. Reset to default returns to that view; Show all assessments clears the filters.
  • Ratings: A Intolerable · B Undesirable · C Acceptable with review · D Acceptable. The rating is calculated from probability and severity and can't be overridden.
  • Most people can view. Editors (usually the ICPM and Senior Engineer) can Create record, Edit and Save to Access. Records are closed, never deleted.

7. Calendar and routines

Calendar

Schedule → Calendar is the shared project calendar. It also shows OpenProject task deadlines.

  • Switch between Month view and Agenda view. The arrows change month and Today jumps back. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday.
  • New event (or click a day) creates one of four kinds of entry:
    • Meeting or Event: goes on the shared calendar, and can repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.
    • Task: creates a real OpenProject task with start and due dates.
    • Reminder: a private reminder for you only.
  • Open an entry to Edit or Cancel event. For a repeating series, choose This occurrence or Whole series.
  • Tags & report settings lets you show or hide tagged entries.

BCW meetings do not send invitations. Tell attendees yourself, or use mail.

Routines

Schedule → Routines holds your repeating jobs, such as a daily check or a weekly return.

  • New routine: give it a title, choose how it repeats (every day, weekdays, selected weekdays or monthly), and set a due time and a reminder.
  • Each routine has Done, Skip, Snooze 1 hour, Pause/Resume, Edit and Delete routine.
  • Missed dates collapse into one overdue item, so there is never a backlog to clear.
  • Reminders can appear in classic Outlook through your private calendar address, shown on the page. Your administrator sets this up per laptop.

8. Weekly reporting

Reporting → Weekly reporting prepares two kinds of report as editable PowerPoint drafts:

  • the client PLOW (Plan of the Week, 16 slides);
  • the internal BMT weekly meeting deck (9 slides).

Your part: the weekly update

  1. Open the current reporting cycle and choose Supply an optional weekly update.
  2. Pick your Discipline, then fill in what applies: Accomplishments this period, Ad hoc support, Next-week plan, Concerns / decisions needed, and Source references / supporting files.
  3. Press Submit weekly update.

If you leave your discipline blank or write "No change", last week's slide text is kept.

What the ICPM does

  1. Creates the reporting cycle.
  2. Attaches last week's PLOW and the FTE hours workbooks.
  3. Asks the AI to prepare the draft. For the internal deck, that is a short interview of about six questions.
  4. Reviews and confirms every statement.
  5. Downloads the PowerPoint.

The output is always a draft: issuing it is a human decision.

9. Files, mail and other tools

Files

  • Tools → Files → Project files: Upload file, Search files and Download copy.
  • To edit an Office file in place, open it in Nextcloud and use Edit locally. A downloaded copy is separate, so changes to it don't save back.
  • Mother documents: you can add new files. Existing Mother files can't be replaced, renamed, moved or deleted from BCW.

Conversations

Tools → Conversations shows the team's Nextcloud Talk rooms. Read and post with Send message. Earlier messages loads older messages.

Daily Brief

Daily Brief prepares the daily team email: personnel movements, schedule, risks, tasks and history.

  • Prepare briefing preview shows the exact message.
  • Send now sends exactly that message to the approved site addresses.
  • Your administrator can set it to send automatically on a schedule.

Nextcloud

The Nextcloud area links to Nextcloud Files and Mail. Nextcloud asks you to sign in with the same username and password.

Resources and deployments

Resources → Deployments ("Deployments & onboarding") shows who is arriving and leaving, and which cubicle, laptop and apartment each person has. Everyone can view it. The ICPM and ICSS plan and activate deployments.

My account

Tools → My account lets you change your password and see your sessions. Sign out all my BCW sessions signs you out everywhere, which is useful if you left yourself signed in on another PC.

11. When something isn't working

"AI is not configured yet", or New session is greyed outThe Assistant isn't connected to the Ascent. Tell the ICPM.
The Assistant is slow or seems stuckTwo others may already be using it; yours is next. If someone switched AI models, the first answer can take up to 3 minutes. Use Interrupt if it's clearly going nowhere.
An answer fails in a very long chatAsk again, or start a New session and summarise what you need.
Image says it is waitingIt is queued, normally for about a minute. If it stays queued much longer, tell the ICPM: the Ascent may be busy or its image service down.
"You already have five image jobs waiting"Let some finish before asking for more.
A notice that chat is paused while images are madeThe ICPM has turned on image pausing. Answers resume in about 3 minutes, and anything you send meanwhile is answered afterwards.

A new Library document doesn't appearWait up to 15 minutes or ask a Library editor to Scan now. Check the file name (DocNo_Rev_Title) and the folder. Scanned files take longer.
